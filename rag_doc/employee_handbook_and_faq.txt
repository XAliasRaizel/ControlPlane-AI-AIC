Employee Handbook — Leave Policy (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
======================================================================
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of annual leave per calendar year,
credited at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Leave requests must
be submitted through the HR Portal at least 3 working days in advance for
requests of 3 days or fewer, and 10 working days in advance for longer
requests. Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5
days into the following calendar year; any balance beyond that is
forfeited unless local law requires otherwise. Sick leave is tracked
separately from annual leave and does not require advance notice, though
a manager should be informed on the first day of absence. Employees on
leave for more than 20 consecutive working days should contact HR to
discuss extended-leave and return-to-work procedures.

Employee Handbook — Working Hours and Remote Work (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
========================================================================================
Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with
a one-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours, during which all
employees are expected to be reachable regardless of flexible scheduling,
are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. Employees may work remotely up to 3
days per week with manager approval; fully remote arrangements require
director-level sign-off and are evaluated on a role-by-role basis.

IT Support FAQ — Account and Password Management (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
========================================================================================
Corporate account passwords must be reset every 90 days and require a
minimum of 12 characters including at least one number and one symbol.
To reset a forgotten password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the
login page; a reset link is emailed within 5 minutes. If no email arrives
within 15 minutes, contact the IT helpdesk directly rather than
requesting repeated reset emails, as repeated requests can trigger a
temporary account lock for security reasons. Multi-factor authentication
is mandatory for all accounts with access to finance or HR systems.

Product Documentation — ControlPlane Governance Actions (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
================================================================================================
ControlPlane's decision engine can take one of five actions on any
governed request: ALLOW (no concerns found), MODIFY (response sanitized
before being returned, e.g. PII redacted), REROUTE (not yet implemented
in this prototype), HUMAN_REVIEW (held for a reviewer to resolve rather
than automatically actioned), and BLOCK (request denied outright). Every
decision, regardless of action, is written to the audit log with the
matched policy rule and the detector scores that produced it.

Finance FAQ — Expense Reimbursement (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
===========================================================================
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date
through the Finance Portal, with itemized receipts attached for any
single expense over $25. Reimbursements are processed on a bi-weekly
cycle and are typically credited within 10 business days of report
approval. Travel expenses require pre-approval from a manager for any
trip exceeding $1,000 in estimated total cost.

Code of Conduct — Workplace Behavior Expectations (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
========================================================================================
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners
with respect and professionalism. Harassment, bullying, and retaliation
against anyone who raises a concern in good faith are prohibited and will
result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Concerns
should be raised with a manager, HR, or through the anonymous ethics
hotline; retaliation for using the hotline is itself a violation of this
policy and is treated as a separate, aggravating disciplinary matter.
Conflicts of interest -- including outside employment, financial
relationships with vendors, and hiring or supervising a family member --
must be disclosed to HR before they arise wherever possible, and no later
than promptly after the employee becomes aware of them.

Equal Employment Opportunity and Anti-Discrimination Policy (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
====================================================================================================
The company is an equal-opportunity employer. Hiring, promotion,
compensation, and performance-evaluation decisions must be based on
job-related qualifications, performance, and business needs, and must not
be influenced by race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, gender
identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, marital or
family status, or any other legally protected characteristic. Any
employee who believes a decision was influenced by a protected
characteristic -- including a decision that involved an AI-assisted
recommendation -- may raise it through HR or the ethics hotline described
in the Code of Conduct section above; such reports are handled under the
same non-retaliation protections. Managers using ControlPlane-governed
tools for hiring or performance-related recommendations should treat a
HUMAN_REVIEW or BLOCK outcome from the bias/fairness detectors as a
required stop-and-review point, not an obstacle to route around.

Data Handling, Confidentiality, and Acceptable Use of AI Tools (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
========================================================================================================
Confidential company information -- including customer data, employee
records, financial results before public release, and source code --
must not be shared outside the company without authorization, and access
should be limited to what an employee's role actually requires. When
using internal AI copilots or external AI tools, employees must not paste
customer PII, unreleased financial figures, or source code into any
AI tool that has not been explicitly approved for that data
classification; approved internal copilots are governed by ControlPlane,
which applies the same data-classification rules (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH) to AI
interactions that already apply to other systems. Employees are
personally responsible for verifying the accuracy of any AI-generated
content (text, code, or analysis) before relying on it or sharing it
externally -- an AI response that passed governance checks has been
screened for policy risk, not certified as factually correct in every
respect.

Health and Wellness Benefits Overview (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
=============================================================================
Full-time employees are eligible for company-subsidized health insurance
starting on the first day of the month following their start date, with
the company covering a majority of the premium for employee-only coverage
and a partial contribution toward dependent coverage. A wellness stipend
of a fixed annual amount is available for gym memberships, fitness
classes, or mental-health app subscriptions, reimbursed through the same
Finance Portal process described in the expense reimbursement section
above. An Employee Assistance Program (EAP) providing confidential
counseling sessions is available at no cost to the employee, independent
of health-insurance enrollment status, and may be accessed directly
without manager notification.

Performance Review Cycle (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
=================================================================
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in mid-year and at
year-end, with the year-end review determining annual compensation
adjustments and bonus eligibility. Each review includes a self-assessment,
manager assessment, and for people-manager roles, upward feedback from
direct reports. Ratings and written feedback are recorded in the HR
system and retained for the duration of employment plus a standard
post-employment retention period for legal and reference purposes.
Employees may request a copy of their own review records at any time
through HR, consistent with the general principle -- also reflected in
the GDPR right-of-access provisions summarized in the regulatory corpus
-- that individuals can access records held about them.

Security Incident Reporting (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
====================================================================
Any suspected security incident -- a lost or stolen device, a phishing
email that was clicked, credentials that may have been compromised, or an
AI system that appears to have surfaced information it should not have --
must be reported to the IT Security team immediately, and in any case
within 24 hours of discovery. Do not attempt to independently investigate
or remediate a suspected breach before reporting it, since early
containment steps taken without security team involvement can destroy
evidence needed for the incident response process. Reports made in good
faith are not subject to disciplinary action even if the employee's own
action (e.g. clicking a phishing link) contributed to the incident;
failing to report a known incident promptly is treated more seriously
than the original mistake.

Business Travel Policy (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
===============================================================
As noted in the expense reimbursement policy, trips estimated to exceed
$1,000 in total cost require manager pre-approval before booking. Flights
should be booked through the corporate travel portal where available, in
economy class for domestic travel under 6 hours and for international
travel under 8 hours; exceptions require director approval. A daily meal
per diem applies for multi-day trips in lieu of itemized meal receipts,
at a rate set by destination city cost-of-living tier. Personal travel
combined with a business trip is permitted but the employee is
responsible for any incremental cost difference, clearly itemized and
excluded from the expense report.

New Hire Onboarding and Equipment Provisioning (Prototype Internal Knowledge Base)
======================================================================================
New employees receive a company laptop and any role-specific equipment
before or on their first day, provisioned by IT based on the equipment
request submitted during the hiring process. Standard onboarding includes
mandatory security-awareness and code-of-conduct training, both due
within the first 2 weeks of employment, and account provisioning for all
role-required systems, which should be complete before the first day
where possible. Remote and hybrid new hires who need home-office
equipment beyond the standard laptop (monitor, desk, chair) can request
it through the same IT equipment request process, subject to a standard
per-employee budget cap; requests above that cap require manager
approval, following the same approval-threshold pattern used for expense
and travel pre-approval elsewhere in this handbook.
